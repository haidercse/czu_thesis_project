# 19 --- Czech University Application Guide

## Objective

Create a practical self-service guide for students applying to Czech
universities.

## Scope

Cover the routine workflow, such as: - Finding suitable programs -
Checking requirements - Understanding deadlines - Preparing documents -
Creating an application - Tracking checklist progress - Following
official university instructions - Understanding common terminology

## Requirements

-   Reuse the platform's actual workflow terminology.
-   Link students to official sources where applicable.
-   Do not present general guidance as university-specific rules.
-   Do not guarantee admission.

## Completion Criteria

A student can use the platform and guide together without needing an
agency for routine application organization.
