# 20 --- Residence Preparation

## Objective

Add general preparation support for students who need
residence/visa-related preparation after admission.

## Scope

This should be a preparation checklist, not immigration/legal advice.

## Requirements

-   Clearly separate university application steps from residence/visa
    preparation.
-   Use official Czech government sources for current requirements.
-   Show source links and verification dates where possible.
-   Allow students to track preparation items.
-   Never guarantee approval.
-   Do not invent required documents or deadlines.

## Future Integration

The feature can later be connected to the student's
admission/application status.

## Completion Criteria

Students have a structured preparation area with clear official-source
references.
