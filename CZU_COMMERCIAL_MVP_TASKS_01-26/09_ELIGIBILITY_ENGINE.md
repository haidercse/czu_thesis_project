# 09 --- Eligibility Engine

## Objective

Add a transparent rule-based eligibility checker using existing student
profile and program data.

## Inputs

Use only data that exists or is explicitly collected, such as: -
Degree - GPA - Field - Language/test information - Country - Other
documented program requirements

## Output

Show: - Eligible - Potentially eligible / needs review - Missing
information - Requirement not met

Use explanations for every result.

## Rules

-   Do not invent requirements.
-   Do not claim guaranteed admission.
-   Distinguish between verified requirements and incomplete data.
-   Keep rules understandable and testable.
-   AI is not required for this task.

## Completion Criteria

A student can see why a program appears eligible or requires further
review.
